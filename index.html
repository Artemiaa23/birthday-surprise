A Letter For You

✦ ✦ ✦

To the man who holds my heart,

Happy Birthday, my love! 🎂💕 And happiest 47th month of being together, 47 months of choosing each other, growing together, and loving each other through every high and low. It feels like it's just yesterday, yet also like I've known you my whole life.

We first met back in Grade 11 as classmates. Everything started with school works and playing Mobile Legends, you'd always invite me to play, and that was how we began talking, little by little. Then came on December 17, 2022, the day I went to church, honestly, i only go to church because I knew you'd be there. 💗 And you, being the sweet gentleman you are, offered to take me home. That same night, at exactly 11:21 PM, we started chatting… and that was it. That was the moment everything changed. We entered our talking stage, and my heart started beating a little faster, just for you.

Then on December 23, out there in the quiet bukid, under the stars and open sky, you looked at me and said those words I'll never forget. "Let's make this official." ✨ And just like that, we became US.

Since then, my favorite days have always been the ones spent with you. I love our simple, perfect moments, cuddling, sharing good food together, watching movies side by side, and traveling to new places hand in hand. It doesn't matter where we go or what we do, as long as I'm with you, I'm happy. Every moment feels like an adventure, and every quiet moment feels like home.

I fell for everything about you. Your eyes that I could get lost in forever, your hair, your voice that calms me instantly… your whole self, your body, your heart, your soul. I love every single part of you. I fell for your kindness, your handsome smile, and the gentle way you treat me. You are everything I ever dreamed of, wrapped in one amazing person.

You didn't just become my boyfriend, you changed all of me. You shaped my heart, softened my edges, and made me the version of myself that I am today. You became my safe place, the one I run to when the world feels too heavy, the one person who makes everything feel okay just by being there. You are my peace, my comfort, and my greatest blessing.

I know we don't always get it right. We fight, we argue, and sometimes we even say things we don't mean, words that hurt, and sometimes even the word "breakup" slips out in anger. But what makes us special is this, we always talk it out. No matter how hard things get, we choose to understand, to forgive, and to stay. We fix what's broken, we hold each other closer, and everything becomes alright again. That's how I know what we have is real, and it's forever.

And so, on your birthday and our 47th month together, this is my promise to you. I want to be with you not just today, not just for another month or year, but for all the years ahead. I want a future filled with more cuddles, more shared meals, more movies, more travels, more laughter, and even more love. I want to keep growing with you, learning with you, and loving you through every season of life.

Happy Birthday, my love. Thank you for 47 beautiful months. Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for being my safe place. Here's to us, today, tomorrow, and always. 🥂💍

✦ ♡ ✦
With all my love, ❤️
Always yours
